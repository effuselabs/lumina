# Lumina: Product Style Guide & Design System

**Version:** 1.0 (Product Brand)
**Date:** August 15, 2025
**Status:** Finalized

---

### 0. Introduction

This document is the official style guide and design system for Lumina, a product by Effuse Labs. Lumina's brand is an expression of the Effuse Labs mission: to bring clarity and empowerment to our users. This guide ensures that the Lumina user experience is consistent, intuitive, and beautiful across every touchpoint.

---

### 1. Brand Strategy

Lumina embodies the **Creator** archetype. It is an innovative, inspiring, and empowering tool that helps its users build their passion.

#### 1.1 Core Purpose

To illuminate the path to growth for salons, barbershops, and independent stylists by replacing patchwork solutions with a single, elegant platform.

#### 1.2 Messaging Pillars

- **For the End User (Salon & Barbershop Owners):**
  - **Core Message:** "Stop managing your business and start building your passion. Lumina handles the administrative burdens—scheduling, payroll, reporting—so you can focus on your craft and your clients."
- **For the AI-Powered Advantage (The Differentiator):**
  - **Core Message:** "Go beyond simple booking. Lumina is your intelligent partner, actively analyzing your data to give you clear, actionable suggestions that illuminate the path to higher revenue and happier clients."

### 2. The Logo

- **Name:** The "Radiant Spark."
- **Description:** A dynamic sunrise or spark shape made of flowing arcs, rendered in a vibrant gold-to-coral gradient. This mark is energetic and represents light, clarity, and growth.
- **Usage Rules:** The same clear space, minimum size, and misuse rules from the Effuse Labs guide apply.

### 3. Colour Palette & Design System

The Lumina palette is designed for a clean, intuitive, and empowering user interface. It combines a vibrant, energetic brand palette with a clear set of functional colors for UI states.

#### 3.1 Primary Brand Colors

- **Lumina Radiant Gradient:** Used for primary calls-to-action (major buttons, onboarding flows) and key moments of insight.
  - Start: `#FFD25A` (Gold)
  - End: `#FF7A5A` (Coral)
- **Primary Accent:** **Solid Lumina Gold (`#FFD25A`)**. The go-to color for most interactive elements.

#### 3.2 Secondary Brand Colors

- **Secondary Accent:** **Solid Deep Teal (`#0B2B33`)**. Used for secondary buttons, containers, and structural elements like sidebars. This color provides a visual link back to the parent Effuse Labs brand.

#### 3.3 Neutral / UI Colors

- **Text:** `#1D2D35` (Off-Black)
- **Secondary Text:** `#808285` (Medium Grey)
- **Borders & Dividers:** `#E4E6E7` (Light Neutral)
- **Backgrounds:** `#F1F3F5` (Light Grey)
- **White:** `#FFFFFF`

#### 3.4 Functional UI Colors

These colors provide clear, immediate feedback to the user and should be used exclusively for their intended function.

- **Success:** A vibrant, accessible green for confirmation messages and "active" states.
  - **Success Green:** `#22C58B`
- **Warning:** A noticeable, warm amber for non-critical alerts or notifications.
  - **Warning Amber:** `#FFB800`
- **Error:** A clear and unambiguous red for failed operations and error messages.
  - **Error Red:** `#E5484D`

### 4. Typography

#### 4.1 Logotype

- **Font Basis:** Montserrat Bold.
- **Style:** All Caps with increased letter-spacing. The 'A' has been customized with a flat top, creating a unique, architectural look. This is a graphic asset and should not be recreated manually.

#### 4.2 UI Typeface

- **Font:** **Inter** (For all headlines, body, and UI text within the application).
- **Weights:** Bold (700) for major headings, SemiBold (600) for sub-headings, Regular (400) for all other text.

Within the Lumina product, our voice is always:

### 7. Social Media Presence (Product-Focused)

Lumina may have its own product-focused channels (e.g., Instagram) in the future.
