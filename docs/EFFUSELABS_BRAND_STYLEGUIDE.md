# Effuse Labs: Brand Style Guide

**Version:** 4.0 (Corporate Brand)
**Date:** August 15, 2025
**Status:** Finalized

---

### 0. Introduction

This document is the official brand style guide for the Effuse Labs corporate brand. Our brand is more than just a logo; it's a promise. This guide ensures that we communicate our story with clarity, consistency, and confidence across all platforms. Our mission is to pour out a continuous stream of innovative ideas and elegant code to empower small businesses.

---

### 1. Brand Strategy

Our brand is the thoughtful fusion of two archetypes: **The Sage & The Creator**. We provide trusted wisdom (Sage) through our innovative tools (Creator).

#### 1.1 Core Values & Personality

- **Values:** Clarity, Empowerment, Innovation, Accessibility.
- **Keywords:** Illuminate, Flow, Growth, Clarity, Effortless, Insight, Radiate, Pour, Potential.
- **Desired Emotional Response:** Users should feel **relieved, confident, and empowered.**

#### 1.2 Tagline

- **Primary Tagline:** Intelligent Software for Small Business Growth.

#### 1.3 Core Messaging Pillars

- **For Our Corporate Identity (Investors, Partners, Future Hires)**
  - **Core Message:** "We believe local businesses are the backbone of our communities. Effuse Labs exists to democratize the power of technology, building intelligent and accessible software that levels the playing field and empowers small enterprises to build resilient, profitable futures."

### 2. The Logos

#### 2.1 Primary Logo

- **Name:** The "Hatching Core."
- **Description:** An abstract, fluid drop shape. An outer shell with a slate-to-teal gradient peels back to reveal a radiant golden orb at its center. It represents the process of revealing valuable insight from complex data.

#### 2.2 Secondary Mark / Favicon

- **Name:** The "Spark" Icon.
- **Description:** A simplified, minimalist icon derived from the "Hatching Core" logo, representing the core 'spark' of insight.
- **Usage:** Favicons, social media profiles, app icons, and compact UI elements where the full logo is not legible.

#### 2.3 Logo Usage Rules

- **Clear Space:** Maintain a minimum clear space around the logos equal to 1/4 of the height of the abstract mark itself.
- **Minimum Size:** Digital: 48px height. Print: 20mm height.
- **Misuse:** Do not stretch, condense, rotate, change colors, add effects, or place on visually cluttered backgrounds.

### 3. Colour Palette

The Effuse Labs palette is built on a sophisticated, premium base that inspires trust and communicates quality.

#### 3.1 Primary Palette

- **Slate Grey:** `#2E3440`
- **Effuse Teal:** `#22C5C3`
- **Lumina Gold (Accent):** `#FFD25A`

#### 3.2 Neutral & Text Palette

- **Off-Black:** `#1D2D35`
- **Medium Grey:** `#808285`
- **Light Grey:** `#F1F3F5`
- **White:** `#FFFFFF`

#### 3.3 Application & Usage

The brand's feel is premium, trustworthy, and sophisticated. The dominant colors for backgrounds and large surfaces should be Slate Grey and the Neutrals. **Effuse Teal** and **Lumina Gold** are to be used as powerful accents for links, buttons, and key highlights.

---

### 4. Typography

#### 4.1 Logotype

- **Font Basis:** Poppins Medium.
- **Style:** Title Case with custom letter-spacing. This is a graphic asset and should not be recreated manually.

#### 4.2 Primary Typeface

- **Font:** **Inter** (For all headlines, body, and UI text).
- **Weights:** Bold (700) for H1, SemiBold (600) for H2, Regular (400) for Body.

#### 4.3 Accent Typeface

- **Font:** **IBM Plex Mono** (Used sparingly for data/code snippets).

---

### 5. Brand Voice & Tone

- **Our Voice is:** Empowering, not prescriptive. Clear, not jargon-filled. Warm, not clinical. Inspiring, not over-hyped.

---

### 6. Imagery Style

- **Photography:** Warm, authentic, and soft-focus. Feature creative professionals in their natural element.
- **Illustration:** Clean, minimalist, and abstract. Use subtle light refractions and fluid shapes that align with our brand aesthetic.

### 8. Social Media Guidelines

The corporate social media presence is the primary voice of Effuse Labs.

#### 8.1 Platform-Specific Tone

- **LinkedIn:** Professional and industry-focused. Authoritative yet approachable.
- **Instagram/Facebook:** Visual and community-focused. The tone is warm and celebratory.
- **Twitter (X) & BlueSky:** Conversational and immediate. The tone is direct and helpful.

#### 8.2 Visual Strategy

- **Profile Pictures:** Use the "Spark" Icon.
- **Templates:** Create a set of branded templates for different post types.
- **Imagery:** Must align with the primary Imagery Style (Section 6).
