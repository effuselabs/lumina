### Decisions Made

#### Decision: Separate EmailQueue from CommunicationHistory

- **Context**: Need both operational queue management and historical tracking
- **Decision**: Create separate EmailQueue model for queue operations, use CommunicationHistory for sent email records
- **Rationale**: 
  - CommunicationHistory is for historical records and analytics
  - EmailQueue is for operational queue management with retry logic
  - Separation of concerns improves maintainability
- **Alternatives Considered**: Extend CommunicationHistory with queue fields
- **Impact**: Cleaner architecture, better performance for queue operations
- **Spec Reference**: `.kiro/specs/notification-system/design.md`

#### Decision: Support Both Default and Business-Specific Templates

- **Context**: Need flexibility for system-wide and customized templates
- **Decision**: EmailTemplate.businessId is optional (nullable)
- **Rationale**: 
  - Default templates (businessId = null) for system-wide use
  - Business-specific templates override defaults
  - Unique constraint on `businessId + type` prevents duplicates
- **Alternatives Considered**: Separate tables for default vs custom templates
- **Impact**: Simpler schema, easier template resolution logic
- **Requirements**: 4.1, 4.2, 4.3

#### Decision: Email Preferences Independent of Client Model

- **Context**: Need to track preferences for emails not yet linked to clients
- **Decision**: EmailPreference.clientId is optional, email is required
- **Rationale**: 
  - Support preferences before client record creation
  - Handle unsubscribes from non-clients
  - Unique constraint on `businessId + email` ensures one preference per email
- **Alternatives Considered**: Require client record for all preferences
- **Impact**: More flexible preference management, supports GDPR compliance
- **Requirements**: 8.1, 8.2, 8.3, 8.4, 8.5
